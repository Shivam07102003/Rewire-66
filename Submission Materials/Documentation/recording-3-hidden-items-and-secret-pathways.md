# 🎮 Rewire 66 — Video 3: Hidden Items and Secret Pathways

## 🧭 Video Focus

This video shows progression from the Control Room through Maintenance, Storage, and Loading Bay, including hidden-component discovery, repair tasks, access cards, and an alternate route into Generator A.

## 📽️ Gameplay Shown

### 🚶 Control Room to Maintenance

- Player travels from the Control Room to Maintenance.
- The connected facility layout and room progression are demonstrated.

### 📡 Hidden Fuse Discovery

- A hidden fuse is located using the radar.
- The fuse is collected and used to repair the Maintenance panel.

### 🎫 Loading Bay Access

- The repaired panel provides the Loading Bay access card.
- The card allows progression toward the Loading Bay area.

### 🔎 Storage Circuit Board

- The radar is used to locate the Storage circuit board from the Entry Room.
- The circuit board is collected for the Storage repair task.

### ⚠️ Recording Clarification: Fake Circuit Board

- The fake circuit board appears again during this video because the recording was restarted.
- This is intentional for the demonstration and is not a gameplay bug during continuous play.
- If the game is played continuously from the earlier videos, the fake circuit board does not respawn or appear again after it has already been encountered.

### 📦 Storage Repair Task

- The collected Storage circuit board is used to complete the Storage task.
- The repair advances the facility restoration sequence.

### 🔌 Loading Bay Wire Task

- The Loading Bay wire task is completed.
- The repaired system allows further progression through the facility.

### 🎫 Generator A Access Card

- The Generator A access card is obtained after completing the required progression.
- The card is used to access the Generator A area.

### 🕳️ Secret Passage to Generator A

- The video demonstrates the intentional secret passage from Loading Bay to Generator A.
- The player enters Generator A through this alternate route.
- From inside Generator A, the player uses the Generator A access card to open the secured area.

## 🛣️ Valid Route Clarification

The Loading Bay → Generator A passage is an intentional alternate route created for exploration and progression.

The player could also travel from the Control Room to Generator A, and the Generator A access card would still work correctly. Both routes are valid:

- **Loading Bay → Secret Passage → Generator A**
- **Control Room → Generator A**

The route shown in this video demonstrates the secret passage, not a required replacement for the Control Room route.
