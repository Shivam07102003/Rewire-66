# 🎮 Rewire 66 — Video 1: Basic Gameplay

## 🧭 Video Focus

This video follows the player’s first major progression through Facility 66: exploring the facility, locating components, completing repairs, surviving a trap encounter, and opening the storage area.

## 📽️ Gameplay Shown

### 🚶 Exploration

- Player movement from the Entry Room into the Central Hub.
- Map opened to identify the player’s position.
- Navigation back toward the starting area.

### 📋 Objectives

- First task and its required component are displayed.
- The next objective is shown after the first repair.
- The Central Hub repair requirement is displayed.

### 📡 Radar and Fuse Search

- Radar categories are opened.
- The component category is used to locate the first fuse.
- The fuse is collected and used for the Entry Room repair.
- The radar is used again to locate the second fuse.
- The fuse count is shown in the inventory.

### 🔧 Facility Repairs

- Entry Room panel repaired with the first fuse.
- Central Hub panel repaired with the second fuse.
- Repairs advance the facility objectives.

### 🎫 Storage Access

- Storage Access Card collected after repairing the Central Hub.
- The card is used to open the Storage Access Gate.
- The opened gate reveals the next area of the facility.

### ⚠️ Trap and Combat

- A fake component trap is triggered in the Central Hub.
- Parasites attack the player.
- The player uses the revolver to defeat them.
- Reloading and bullet backfire are demonstrated.
- Kept a mechanism where bullets spawn at random for every room.

### ❤️ Healing

- The player begins healing after taking damage.
- Healing stops when the player starts moving.

## ✅ Systems Demonstrated

- Player movement and exploration
- Map navigation
- Objective and requirement UI
- Radar categories
- Component tracking
- Fuse pickups and inventory counts
- Repair panels
- Access-card progression
- Locked gate authorization
- Fake component traps
- Parasite combat
- Revolver reloading
- Bullet backfire
- Healing interruption while moving

The video ends with the Storage Access Gate open, allowing the player to continue deeper into Facility 66.
