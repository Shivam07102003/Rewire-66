# 🎮 Rewire 66 — Video 2: Blackout and Old Findings

## 🧭 Video Focus

This video follows the player’s progress through the Storage area, where a blackout occurs and the player restores power, obtains access authorization, opens a secured gate, and repairs the Control Panel.

## 📽️ Gameplay Shown

### 📦 Storage Entry

- Player enters the Storage area through the gate opened in Video 1.
- The newly accessible section of Facility 66 is explored.

### 🌑 Blackout Event

- The facility enters a blackout after the player reaches Storage.
- The area becomes dark and more difficult to navigate.

### 🔌 Wire Repair

- The player completes the wire repair task.
- The repair restores the facility lights.
- The change from blackout to restored lighting is shown.

### 💡 Power Restoration

- The lights return after the wire task is completed.
- The repaired system changes the environment and allows progression.

### 📡 Access-Card Radar Filter

- The radar filter menu is opened.
- The filter is used to display only access-card locations.
- The access card is found and collected.

### 🧰 Inventory Requirement

- The player already has the required Control Board in the inventory.
- This demonstrates that previously collected items remain available for later objectives.

### 🚪 Gate Authorization

- The player approaches the secured gate.
- The access card authorizes the gate.
- The gate opens and allows the player to enter the next area.

### 🖥️ Control Panel Repair

- The Control Board is used to repair the Control Panel.
- The completed repair advances the restoration of Facility 66.
