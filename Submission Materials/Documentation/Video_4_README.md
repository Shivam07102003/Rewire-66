# 🎮 Rewire 66 — Video 4: Gen A,B & Locked Compartment

## 🧭 Video Focus

This video follows the Generator A and Generator B progression chain. The player finds a hidden control board, repairs Generator A, obtains access to Generator B, and completes the Generator B fuse repair.

## 📽️ Gameplay Shown

### ⚙️ Generator A — First Repair

- The player begins in the Generator A area.
- The first repair requires the Generator A Control Board.
- The control board is hidden in Maintenance.
- The radar is used to locate the hidden component.
- The Generator A panel is repaired.

### 🔑 Loading Bay Key

- A key spawns in the Loading Bay after the Generator A repair.
- The key is collected for the next progression step.

### 🔒 Generator A Locked Compartment

- The Loading Bay key is used to open the locked compartment inside Generator A.
- The compartment contains the Generator B Access Card.

### 🎫 Generator B Access

- The Generator B Access Card is collected.
- The Generator B gate is opened using the access card.

### 🔥 Generator B — Fuse Repair

- The player enters the Generator B area.
- The required fuse repair task is completed.
- The repair advances the restoration of the facility’s power systems.
