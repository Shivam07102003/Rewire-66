# 🌐 RE:WIRE 66 — General Lore

## 🏷️ Meaning of the Title

The full form of **RE:WIRE 66** is:

> **Wasteland In Route End 66**

The title also represents the game’s main objective: to rewire and restore the abandoned facility’s broken electrical systems at Route End 66.

## 📍 The Setting

RE:WIRE 66 takes place in an old, abandoned communications facility in the Wasteland at Route End 66. The facility has not been used for years, leaving its electrical systems, gates, repair panels, and communications equipment inactive.

The player becomes stranded inside the facility with no reliable way to contact the outside world. The only way to call for help is to restore the facility and reactivate its communications tower.

## 🤖 The Player

The player controls a small repair robot. The robot must explore the facility, locate missing components, repair damaged systems, open restricted areas, and survive the parasites attracted to the returning electrical power.

The facility includes the Entry Room, Central Hub, Maintenance, Storage, Loading Bay, Control Room, Generator A, and Generator B.

## 🔧 The Mission

The robot must:

- Explore the abandoned facility.
- Locate hidden and visible components.
- Collect fuses, circuit boards, wire sets, coolant, batteries, access cards, and keys.
- Repair damaged electrical panels and wire systems.
- Open locked compartments and authorized gates.
- Restore the generator areas.
- Activate the communications tower.
- Survive the final transmission long enough to send a rescue signal.

## ⚡ Key Gameplay Functionality

### 📡 Radar and Hidden Components

- The radar displays selected categories such as components, batteries, access cards, keys, and parasites.
- Some components are hidden or difficult to see in the environment.
- Hidden components appear on the radar before they are visible in the world.
- The component becomes visible or collectible when the robot moves close enough to it.
- Radar categories help the player search for one type of item without unnecessary map clutter.

### 🗺️ Map Navigation

- The facility map shows the connected rooms and the robot’s position.
- The player can use the map to return to previous areas and plan routes between objectives.

### 🔋 Randomized Ammunition

- Bullet or ammunition pickups spawn in different locations during different games.
- The player cannot rely on exactly the same ammunition route every time.
- Radar exploration and resource management are therefore important during combat.

### 💥 Probabilistic Bullet Backfire

- The revolver has a probability of backfiring when fired.
- A backfire can empty the weapon, damage the robot, and temporarily stun it.
- The player must balance the need to fight parasites against the risk of using the weapon.

### ❤️ Healing Interruption

- Healing requires the robot to remain still.
- Moving before healing finishes interrupts the healing process.
- The player must choose a safe moment to recover during exploration and combat.

### ⚠️ Traps and Parasites

- Some components are fake and act as traps instead of useful pickups.
- Parasites are attracted to the robot, batteries, electrical systems, alarms, and restored power.
- The player must decide whether to fight, heal, reload, escape, or continue repairing.

### 🗝️ Keys, Access Cards, and Gates

- Keys and access cards are stored as separate inventory items.
- Access cards authorize specific facility gates.
- Keys open specific locked compartments or fence gates.
- Some rewards and new areas only become available after completing earlier repairs.

### 🔧 Repair Progression

- Repairs require specific components.
- Completing a task can restore lights, remove a blackout, spawn a key, provide an access card, or unlock a new area.
- The objective system shows the next task and its required component.

## 👾 The Parasites

The facility is occupied by hostile, energy-feeding parasites. They become a greater threat as more systems are restored and more electrical equipment becomes active.

The revolver allows the robot to defend itself, but limited ammunition, backfire probability, healing interruption, and parasite attacks make every encounter dangerous.

## 📈 Overall Progression

```text
Explore the Facility
        ↓
Use the Radar to Find Components
        ↓
Repair Electrical Systems
        ↓
Unlock Gates and Compartments
        ↓
Restore Generator Areas
        ↓
Activate the Communications Tower
        ↓
Survive the Final Transmission
```

## 📡 The Final Transmission

After the major systems are restored, the robot reaches the communications tower in Generator B and begins the final transmission.

The Generator B and fence doors lock during the sequence. The robot must survive for 30 seconds while three large parasites pursue it. Defeating the parasites and surviving the full timer completes the transmission and displays the victory message.

## 🏆 Possible Outcomes

### ✅ Victory

The robot survives the final transmission, sends the rescue signal, and receives the victory message.

### 💀 Game Over

If the robot is defeated during the final battle, the transmission fails and the Game Over message appears.
