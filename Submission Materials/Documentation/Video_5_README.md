# 🎮 Rewire 66 — Video 5: Generators, Keys and Final Volley

## 🧭 Video Focus

This video shows the final facility-repair chain and the communications-tower survival sequence. The player restores the remaining Generator A and Generator B systems before attempting to send a signal for help.

## 📽️ Gameplay Shown

### 🔑 Generator B Fence Key

- Completing the Generator B fuse task spawns the Generator B fence key.
- The key is collected and used to open the fence gate between Generator A and Generator B.

### 🧊 Generator A Coolant Repair

- The player travels to Storage to collect a coolant canister.
- The coolant is used for the second Generator A task.

### 🖥️ Hidden Generator B Control Board

- The next requirement is a Generator B Control Board.
- The board is hidden in Loading Bay.
- The radar is used to locate and collect it.

### 🔌 Loading Bay Repair

- The Loading Bay task is completed using the required control board.
- Completing the task spawns the Storage locker key in the Control Room.

### 🔒 Storage Locker Key

- The Storage locker key is collected from the Control Room.
- The key opens the locked compartment.
- The compartment contains the coolant canister required for the final Generator A task.

### 🧊 Final Generator A Task

- The coolant canister is taken to Generator A.
- The final Generator A coolant task is completed.

### 📡 Communications Tower — Final Volley

- The player travels to the Generator B communications tower.
- The tower is activated to begin the final volley task.
- The Generator B and fence doors lock during the sequence.
- The player must survive for 30 seconds.
- Three large parasites pursue the player.
- The parasites are defeated while the survival timer continues.
- The player survives the task and the victory message appears.
