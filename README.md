# 🌐 RE:WIRE 66 — Game Lore and Gameplay Overview

## 🏷️ Meaning of the Title

The full form of **RE:WIRE 66** is:

> **Wasteland In Route End 66**

The title also represents the game’s central objective: rewiring and restoring the abandoned electrical systems at Route End 66.

## 📍 The Setting

**RE:WIRE 66** takes place inside an abandoned communications facility in the Wasteland at Route End 66. The facility has been unused for years, leaving its lights, gates, repair panels, generators, and communications equipment inactive.

The player becomes stranded inside the facility with no reliable way to contact the outside world. To call for help, the player must restore the facility’s electrical systems, reach the communications tower in Generator B, and complete the final transmission.

The facility includes the Entry Room, Central Hub, Maintenance Room, Storage Room, Loading Bay, Control Room, Generator A, and Generator B.

## 🤖 The Player and Main Objective

The player controls a small repair robot. The robot must explore the facility, locate missing components, repair damaged systems, unlock restricted areas, manage limited resources, and survive hostile parasites attracted by the returning electrical power.

The main mission is to:

- 🗺️ Explore the abandoned facility.
- 🔎 Locate visible and hidden components.
- 🧰 Collect fuses, circuit boards, wire sets, coolant canisters, batteries, access cards, and keys.
- 🔧 Repair damaged electrical panels and wire systems.
- 🚧 Open locked compartments and authorized gates.
- ⚡ Restore Generator A and Generator B.
- 📡 Activate the communications tower.
- 👾 Survive the final transmission.
- 📶 Send the rescue signal.

## ⚡ Core Gameplay Systems

### 📡 Radar and Hidden Components

The radar displays selected categories of objects, including components, battery packs, access cards, keys, parasites, and other important objects. Some components are hidden or difficult to see in the environment. They can appear on the radar before they are visible in the world and become visible or collectible when the robot moves close enough.

The radar filter allows the player to search for one category at a time, keeping the map clear and helping the player focus on the item required for the current objective.

### 🗺️ Map Navigation

The facility map shows the connected rooms and the robot’s position. It helps the player understand the layout, return to earlier rooms, plan routes, locate gates and repair areas, and reach newly opened sections of the facility.

### 🔧 Repairs and Progression

Repairs are the main form of progression. Each repair requires a specific component. Completing a task can restore lights, remove a blackout, unlock a gate, open a new area, spawn a key, provide an access card, reveal a new objective, or activate additional facility systems.

The objective system displays the current task and the component required to complete it. Every repair brings the robot closer to the communications tower, but restored power also makes the facility more active and dangerous.

### 🗝️ Keys, Access Cards, and Locked Areas

Keys and access cards are separate inventory items with different purposes. Access cards authorize specific facility gates, while keys open specific locked compartments or fence gates. Some rewards and areas become available only after an earlier repair has been completed.

The player must remember where locked areas are located and return after finding the required key, access card, or repair reward.

### 🔋 Randomized Ammunition

Ammunition pickups can spawn in different locations during different games. Because the player cannot depend on the same ammunition route every time, each playthrough may require different exploration and resource decisions.

This encourages the player to use the radar, search carefully, manage ammunition, and avoid unnecessary combat.

### 🔫 Revolver and Probabilistic Bullet Backfire

The robot uses a revolver to defend itself against parasites, but every shot has a probability of backfiring. A backfire can empty the weapon, damage the robot, or temporarily stun it, leaving the player vulnerable.

The player must balance the need to defeat parasites against the risk of firing at the wrong moment.

### ❤️ Healing Interruption

Healing requires the robot to remain completely still. If the player moves before the healing process finishes, the healing is interrupted. The player must find a safe moment to heal while also deciding whether to fight, reload, escape, or continue the current repair objective.

### ⚠️ Fake Components and Traps

Not every component found in the facility is genuine. Some components are fake and act as traps instead of providing the required repair item. They can waste time, attract parasites, or place the robot in danger.

### 👾 Parasites

The facility is occupied by hostile, energy-feeding parasites. They are attracted to the robot, battery packs, electrical systems, alarms, restored power, and active facility equipment. As more systems are restored, the parasite threat increases.

The robot must use movement, the revolver, healing, reloading, and careful resource management to survive.

## 📡 The Final Transmission

The communications tower is located in Generator B. After the major repair chain is completed, the robot activates the tower and begins the final transmission.

During this sequence, the Generator B doors and fence gates lock. A 30-second survival timer begins, and three large parasites pursue the robot inside the restricted area. The player must defeat or avoid the parasites until the timer ends.

If the robot survives, the transmission is completed and the victory message appears. If the robot is defeated, the transmission fails and the Game Over message appears.

## 🏆 Possible Outcomes

### ✅ Victory

The robot survives the final transmission, sends the rescue signal, and receives the victory message.

### 💀 Game Over

If the robot is defeated during the final battle, the transmission fails and the Game Over message appears. The player must restart the facility’s repair sequence and try again.

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Experience

RE:WIRE 66 combines exploration, repair tasks, radar-based searching, randomized ammunition, resource management, locked-area progression, and survival combat. The game is built around a central risk: restoring the facility brings the robot closer to rescue, but every repaired system also makes the facility more active and attracts more parasites.

The player must balance progress with survival until the final transmission is successfully completed.
